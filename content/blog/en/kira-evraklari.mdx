Renting a home means pulling together many documents in a short time: ID, proof of income, guarantor papers, the lease… and some of them are **sensitive** (income, national ID) while others carry **legal risk** (deposit amount, eviction undertaking). This guide covers **what to prepare as a tenant**, common mistakes, and how to keep the whole file organized in **one bundle.**

> ⚠️ This article is general information, **not legal advice.** For contracts and undertakings, consult a lawyer/notary if in doubt.

## Documents typically requested from a tenant

- **ID** — national ID card (front and back).
- **Proof of income** — employee: payslip + social-security record; self-employed: tax registration; retiree: pension statement.
- **Residence certificate** — obtained from e-Devlet (Turkey's e-government portal).
- **Guarantor documents** (if required) — the guarantor's ID and proof of income.
- **Lease agreement** — signed by both parties (usually two copies).
- **Deposit receipt** — proof the deposit was paid.
- **For utility accounts:** DASK (mandatory earthquake insurance) policy, electricity/water/gas subscription documents.

## Watch out for the deposit and eviction undertaking

These two are the most common sources of dispute:

- **Deposit.** Under the Turkish Code of Obligations, the security deposit for residential leases is **at most 3 months' rent** (Art. 342). If more is requested, you have the right to object.
- **Eviction undertaking.** For validity, the **date of issue should be after the start of the tenancy** (per Court of Cassation precedent). Avoid signing one with a blank date.

These seem minor but cause serious problems later; read before you sign.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can the landlord request any document?

Reasonable and proportionate documents can be requested, but be careful with excessive demands for sensitive data. Redact unnecessary fields when sharing.

### Should the app draft the lease?

No. DocuVault does **not** produce the lease as signature-ready legal text; it only offers an informational **sample** and warnings. For the official contract the parties agree, with a lawyer/notary if needed.

### How do I send documents to the agent securely?

One PDF via "Package & Share," or an expiring/revocable **secure link** sent over WhatsApp. The recipient opens it in a browser without installing the app; you can redact sensitive fields before sharing.

### Where do I get the residence certificate?

From **e-Devlet** — get the "Residence and Other Address Document" instantly, then scan it into your capsule.
