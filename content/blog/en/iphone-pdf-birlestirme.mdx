"Can you send the 3 pages of the contract as **one PDF**?" — we've all heard it. Sending separate photos or five separate PDFs looks messy and makes more work for the other side. The right move: **combine multiple documents into one clean PDF.**

This guide covers the practical ways to merge PDFs on iPhone — and how to do it in the **fewest steps.**

## Method 1: The iOS Files app (simple but limited)

iPhone's built-in **Files** app offers basic merging:

1. Put the PDFs you want to merge into the **same folder** in Files.
2. Tap **Select** (top right) → mark the PDFs.
3. Use **"Create PDF"** or the merge option from the menu.

**Limits:**
- Ordering is fiddly; it sorts by file name.
- Combining images (JPG/HEIC) and PDFs **together** cleanly isn't easy.
- No separator pages, numbering, or naming.
- The document isn't **encrypted/secured**; it just sits there as a plain file.

Fine for a one-off. But if you regularly bundle paperwork (applications, contracts, invoice sets), there's something better.

## Method 2: Merge as you scan (multi-page)

Often the need to merge comes from scanning **multiple pages of the same document.** In DocuVault's built-in scanner:

1. Green **"+"** → **Scan Document**.
2. Capture the first page → **"Add Page"** → second page → "Add Page" again…
3. Drag to reorder pages.
4. **"Finish"** → it all becomes **one PDF**, with perspective and contrast corrected.

That way a 3-page contract enters your vault as a single file from the start.

## Method 3: Bundle different documents in a "Capsule"

The truly powerful scenario: combining **different documents** (passport + insurance + bank statement) into one submission bundle. DocuVault's **Capsules** feature reduces this to a single step:

1. Create a **Capsule** (e.g. "Visa Application" or "Rental File").
2. Add the relevant documents — scan new ones or pick from your vault.
3. Tap **"Package & Share."** DocuVault:
   - Merges all documents (even a mix of PDFs and images) into **one PDF**,
   - Adds a **numbered separator page** before each document (like "01 · Passport"),
   - Gives the file a **meaningful name.**
4. Share the result or save it to your vault.

So instead of "open a folder in Files → sort → merge → rename," it's one tap.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does merging require internet?

Multi-page scanning and packaging documents into one PDF happen on the device. But since your documents are stored encrypted in the cloud, adding/syncing new documents and features like AI summaries require internet.

### Can I merge a PDF and a photo together?

Yes. In capsule packaging, PDFs and images (JPG/HEIC) combine cleanly into one PDF; images are normalized to PDF pages.

### How do I change page order?

By dragging pages while scanning; in a capsule, by arranging the documents in the order you want. The package preserves that order.

### What if the merged file is too large?

Scan outputs are optimized, but many high-resolution pages will grow the file. If needed, split the document into two separate packages.
